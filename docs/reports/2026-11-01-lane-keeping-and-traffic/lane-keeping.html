A bug this exposed

On route 8 the first run followed SimLingo from the first tick, on a straight road, 2.75 m off the lane. The lane lookup asked the map for height 0, but Town12's roads are 300–380 m up, so CARLA returned the oncoming lane. The NMPC steered towards that lane and left the road. With the old path, on the same bug, the car stopped after 9 m. The lookup now uses the car's height, which cut wrong lanes off junctions from 19 % to 4 % of Town12's road points.

How it decides

  1. Lane change or turn: SimLingo's line, from 8 m ahead, is more than 1 m off the map lane. This ends when it is back within 0.5 m. Measuring against the lane, not the car's heading, means a bend in the road doesn't count.
  2. "Straight" means the lane's centre, so the car follows bends in the road.
  3. Stopping: SimLingo's speed counts only if it drops under 1 m/s within 2 s. Otherwise the target is the speed limit, and actors and rules bring it down.
  4. The thresholds held up: the largest offset in lane keeping was 0.57 m across the four routes, and real manoeuvres showed 2.6–2.7 m. Route 10 never hands over, because the map lane already follows its left turn.

Still open

  1. Waiting for good in traffic. This is now the main blocker, and it also happens on the old path on routes 1 and 10. On route 8, notes like "crosser in the lane to inf" come from the moving stream running down the exit lane, which gets read as crossing traffic. An earlier version of this page blamed cars at rest in a queue, and that was wrong.
  2. The kink remains while following SimLingo. The path is still forced through the car during turns and lane changes, so the weave can return there.
  3. A hang on route 8. One run stopped ticking at 38.75 s and sat for an hour, on the old lane lookup. The cause is unknown, and it didn't happen again in the one later run.
